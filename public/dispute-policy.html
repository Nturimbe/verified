✓ Verified — Dispute Policy

Last updated: June 2026

How to Raise a Dispute

Either the buyer or seller may raise a dispute at any point before funds are released.

Buyer: On your confirmation page, tap "There is a problem" instead of confirming receipt.
Seller: On your dispatch page, tap "Raise a Problem" if the item was returned or the buyer is unresponsive.

What Happens When a Dispute Is Raised

Funds are frozen immediately. Neither party can withdraw or release the funds. Both parties receive an SMS notification. The Verified team is alerted automatically.

Response Window

The party who did not raise the dispute has 48 hours to submit their response and evidence. If no response is received within 48 hours, the dispute defaults in favour of the party who raised it.

Evidence We Accept

Photos of the item received, waybills or dispatch confirmations, screenshots of the original product listing, and written descriptions of what was agreed. Evidence must be submitted through the dispute form.

Resolution Timeline

Verified aims to resolve all disputes within 48 hours of receiving both parties' submissions. Complex cases may take up to 5 working days.

Decisions

All decisions are made by the Verified team and are final. Decisions are permanently logged with a reason and the name of the admin who decided. Transactions above GHS 500 require sign-off from two members of the Verified team.

Outcomes

Release to Seller: Funds are released to the seller's MoMo. The buyer receives an SMS confirming the decision.
Refund to Buyer: Funds are returned to the buyer. Note: Paystack's processing fee (1.95%) is non-refundable and will be deducted from the refund amount.

Auto-Release

If a buyer does not confirm receipt and does not raise a dispute within 72 hours of the seller marking dispatch, funds are automatically released to the seller.
