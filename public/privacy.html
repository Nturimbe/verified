✓ Verified — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use Verified we collect: your name, phone number, email address, Mobile Money number (partially masked in all displays), transaction details including item name and amount, and device/browser information for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to process transactions, send SMS notifications, resolve disputes, and comply with Ghanaian financial regulations. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Transaction data is stored securely on encrypted cloud infrastructure. Mobile Money numbers are masked in all user-facing displays. We retain transaction records for a minimum of 7 years as required by Ghanaian financial law.

4. SMS Notifications

By providing your phone number you consent to receive SMS notifications about your transaction status. You may not opt out of transactional SMS as they are essential to the escrow service.

5. Third Parties

We share minimal data with Paystack Ghana (our licensed payment partner) for payment processing, and Africa's Talking for SMS delivery. Both are bound by their own privacy policies and Ghanaian data protection requirements.

6. Your Rights

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at privacy@verified.gh to exercise these rights.
