How It Works

I Am a Seller
I Am a Buyer

Sell with confidence.

Generate a payment link, share it on WhatsApp, and get paid only when your buyer confirms they received the item.

🔗

Step 1 — Create Your Link

Go to the Create page. Enter your item name, price, and MoMo number. Click Generate. You get a secure payment link instantly.

💬

Step 2 — Share on WhatsApp

Copy the link and paste it into your WhatsApp, Facebook, or Instagram chat with your buyer. That is it.

💰

Step 3 — Buyer Pays

Your buyer opens the link and pays via MoMo. You receive an SMS immediately confirming the funds are secured. You can now dispatch safely.

📦

Step 4 — Dispatch the Item

Tap the dispatch link in your SMS. Confirm you have sent the item. Your buyer receives an SMS with a confirmation link.

✓

Step 5 — Get Paid

Once the buyer confirms receipt, funds are released instantly to your MoMo. Transaction complete.

What if the buyer does not confirm? If the buyer does not confirm or raise a dispute within your delivery window, funds are released to you automatically. You are always protected.

Buy without the fear.

Your money is protected until you confirm your item arrived exactly as described. If anything goes wrong, Verified steps in.

🔗

Step 1 — Open the Payment Link

The seller sends you a Verified payment link on WhatsApp. Open it and you see the item details, price, and the escrow guarantee.

📱

Step 2 — Pay via MoMo

Enter your email or WhatsApp number and tap Pay. You are redirected to a secure Paystack checkout. Pay with your MoMo.

🔒

Step 3 — Funds Are Held Safely

Your money goes to Verified — not to the seller. The seller cannot access it until you confirm delivery.

📦

Step 4 — Receive Your Item

When the seller dispatches, you get an SMS with a confirmation link. Check your item carefully before confirming.

✓

Step 5 — Confirm and Release

Happy with your item? Tap confirm. Funds are released to the seller. If there is a problem, tap "There is a problem" instead and we step in.

Is my money safe if something goes wrong? Yes. If you raise a dispute before confirming, your funds freeze immediately. The Verified team reviews the evidence from both sides and makes a fair decision within 48 hours.

Common Questions

Does Verified work on WhatsApp?
Yes. The payment link works in any chat app — WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM. No app download needed.
What MoMo networks are supported?
MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo Money. Card payments are also accepted.
How much does Verified charge?
Verified charges a small platform fee (1.5–3%) on successfully completed transactions only. There is no fee for refunded transactions.
How long does it take to receive payment?
Funds are released to the seller's MoMo instantly after the buyer confirms receipt.
What if the buyer does not respond?
If the buyer neither confirms nor raises a dispute within the delivery window, funds auto-release to the seller.
