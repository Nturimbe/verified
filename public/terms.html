1. What Verified Does

Verified is a transaction escrow service. When a buyer pays through Verified, funds are held securely until the buyer confirms receipt of the item or service. Only then are funds released to the seller.

2. Who Can Use Verified

Verified is available to any person in Ghana with a valid Mobile Money account. By using Verified you confirm you are at least 18 years old and that the transaction is for a lawful purpose.

3. Fees

Verified charges a platform fee of 1.5–3% on each successfully completed transaction. This fee is deducted before funds are released to the seller. There are no fees for disputed transactions that result in a refund to the buyer.

4. Escrow and Fund Holding

When a buyer pays, funds are held in escrow by Verified through our licensed payment partner (Paystack Ghana). Funds are released to the seller only after the buyer confirms receipt, or after the automatic release window (72 hours after dispatch) has elapsed without a dispute.

5. Disputes

Either party may raise a dispute before funds are released. Verified will review all disputes within 48 hours. Decisions are final and logged permanently. Verified reserves the right to request evidence from either party.

6. Prohibited Transactions

Verified may not be used for illegal goods or services, financial fraud, or any transaction that violates Ghanaian law. Verified reserves the right to freeze and report any suspicious transaction to relevant authorities.

7. Limitation of Liability

Verified is a neutral intermediary. We are not responsible for the quality, legality, or delivery of items transacted through our platform beyond our escrow obligation. Our maximum liability in any dispute is the transaction amount held in escrow.
